┌─ KEY FORMULAS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  Safety Stock = Z × σ × √LT                                                │
│    Z = Z-score (1.65 for 95%)                                              │
│    σ = Standard deviation of demand                                        │
│    LT = Lead time                                                          │
│                                                                             │
│  Reorder Point = (Avg Daily Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock             │
│                                                                             │
│  EOQ = √((2 × Annual Demand × Ordering Cost) / Holding Cost)               │
│                                                                             │
│  Days of Supply = On-Hand Inventory / Avg Daily Demand                     │
│                                                                             │
